HaNotes — Notes & Handwriting

A notebook app for students: handwriting with a pen, typed notes, PDFs and recordings in one place.

  • Pen input, PDF import and annotation, book-style page turning, audio recording, tasks and a focus timer.
  • Password-locked notebooks stored encrypted; Google Drive backup and restore.
