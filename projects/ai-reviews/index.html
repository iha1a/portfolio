Review Insights AI

Paste customer reviews and a transformer language model, running entirely on your device, scores the sentiment of every sentence and groups the feedback by topic, so a shop owner sees in seconds what customers love and what to fix.

  • Transformers.js
  • DistilBERT (fine-tuned SST-2)
  • ONNX Runtime · WebAssembly
  • Aspect-based sentiment
  • Web Worker-free, no server

The model (≈ 65 MB) downloads once on first use, then is cached by the browser.

How it works

1. Split

Each review is split into sentences, because one review often praises one thing and criticises another.

2. Classify

DistilBERT, a compressed BERT model fine-tuned for sentiment, scores each sentence as positive or negative with a confidence.

3. Detect topics

A keyword lexicon tags each sentence with aspects such as shade, texture, longevity, packaging, price and delivery.

4. Aggregate

Scores are averaged per aspect (−100 to +100) and the most confident complaints are surfaced first.
