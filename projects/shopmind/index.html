How the AI works

Embeddings

Every product description is turned into a 384-number vector by the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence-transformer, in your browser.

Semantic search

Your request becomes a vector too; products are ranked by cosine similarity, so "keep coffee hot" finds a thermos.

Constraint parsing

Budgets like "under $60" and categories are extracted from the sentence and applied as filters.

Recommendations

"Similar items" and "complete your cart" use the same vectors to suggest what fits together.
