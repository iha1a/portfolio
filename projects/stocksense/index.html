      How the system decides

      Forecast

      Holt's double exponential smoothing models level and trend. Its two smoothing factors are tuned automatically per product by grid search on past errors.

      Anomalies

      Weeks whose forecast error is more than 2.5 standard deviations from normal are flagged, so a one-off spike doesn't distort planning.

      Safety stock

      z × σ × √lead time, where σ is the forecast error and z comes from the chosen service level (90% → 1.28 … 99% → 2.33).

      Order size

      Economic order quantity √(2 × annual demand × order cost ÷ holding cost) balances ordering and storage costs.
